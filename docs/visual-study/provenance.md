## Prompt final — produto

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: art-direction study for a premium consumer hardware website, strictly conceptual, not a production asset. Primary request: create one extraordinary photorealistic studio photograph of believable near-future AI smart glasses. No text, no logos, no branding, no collage. Scene: perfectly plain very dark warm graphite studio background (#101110), a subtle horizontal ground plane. Subject: one pair of elegant sculptural matte graphite acetate glasses with modest thick temples that can plausibly house electronics, refined rectangular soft rounded lenses, tiny discreet circular camera on outer corner, real hinges, clear slightly smoke gray lenses with very subtle optical reflections. Nothing sci-fi. Composition: widescreen landscape 16:9, complete glasses large and central, gently elevated three-quarter front view with right temple receding; entire silhouette visible, substantial negative space on all edges, suitable to crop for a mobile composition. Camera nearly at product height, convincing perspective and dimensional construction. Lighting: soft long ivory stripbox from upper left emphasizes softly beveled frame edges, very subtle cool rim at rear, moody realistic shadow beneath glasses. Materials: fine matte acetates, believable lens thickness and natural glass transmission. Premium editorial hardware campaign, quiet precise sophisticated. Avoid floating holograms, neon, colored glows, futuristic visors, extreme sports eyewear, exaggerated bloom, any third-party marks, typography, UI, diagrams, extra products, artificial generative distortions.

## Prompt final — cena

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: conceptual background plate for an art-direction presentation of a premium AI smart-glasses website, no interface or typography in the generated image. Create a calm and highly realistic architectural street photograph from a pedestrian's eye level, looking down a quiet narrow European neighborhood street, pale warm limestone buildings, a small tasteful unbranded corner café on the right with dark graphite metal window frames, one outdoor table, a little greenery, soft late-afternoon daylight and natural shadows. A distant pedestrian may be tiny and unidentifiable. Composition: wide landscape 16:9, strong subtle depth, street opening toward the left third, café facade to the right, a plain cream paper menu inside the window containing no visible readable text, clear foreground sightline. Make it an everyday place someone wants to explore, real photographic materiality, nuanced desaturated warm neutrals and restrained deep green. Editorial travel photography, gentle contrast and no oversaturated sky. No signage, no readable text, no logos, no cars dominating foreground, no fake landmark, no futuristic objects, no augmented reality, no overlaid UI, no bokeh covering important architectural detail, no exaggerated wide-angle distortion. This is a fictional place, not a named real building.
